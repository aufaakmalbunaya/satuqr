04 · How it works

Three steps, two worlds.

  1. 01

    Merchant creates an invoice

    The rupiah amount converts to USDC at the Frankfurter rate. The QRIS payload is built from the shop static string, or a DEMO payload when empty.

  2. 02

    Customer picks how to pay

    Rupiah customers scan QRIS as usual. Crypto customers pick an origin asset, and NEAR Intents 1Click returns an EXACT_OUTPUT quote into USDC on NEAR.

  3. 03

    Merchant receives USDC

    USDC lands in satuqr.near. The invoice status flips to paid. The merchant sees every invoice in the dashboard and exports a CSV.

Honest about this demo

There is no live QRIS acquirer integration yet. That needs a MID and a bank partnership. The rupiah rail uses the merchant own static QRIS and is confirmed manually. The rupiah-to-crypto rail uses a simulated off-ramp in the demo. Intents only runs on mainnet, so quotes here are dry-run and no funds move. 1Click fee: 0.01% for stablecoin pairs with an API key, 0.20% for other pairs. The solver spread sits inside the quote.
